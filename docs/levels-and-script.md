# Ichigo: Levels and Script

*Exported 22 Nov 2026 from the Claude doc: https://claude.ai/artifact/TYjAzR9HUbgTkLrhpk7TZ4 (the doc is the live copy; this is a snapshot for project context).*

## Overview

Ichigo is an adventure in 15 episodes, and each episode is a different kind of game, held together by one child, one sea and one rule: failure is only ever a new tomorrow. Level 1 is the rocking-wreckage platformer already built; the other 14 change genre the way a seven-year voyage changes a child. Level 14 returns to the platformer so the player feels how much Ichigo has grown since Level 1.

Everything here builds on `claude/ichigo-game-design.md` (pillars, aging table, art direction, Odyssey outline). Anything not in the novel is our invention; the novel fixes only the premise, the 15 levels, the aging, the bucket and shovel, the Hokusai look and the homecoming where Ichigo's parents don't recognize them.

**How each level is written up**

- **Card:** the level title card, shown as a kanji numeral and "N / 15" in the cover lettering.
- **Game type, goal, controls, stakes:** what the player does, what can go wrong, and what failure costs (always just a sunrise and a retry from the last carp streamer).
- **Beats:** the level's flow in order.
- **What Ichigo leaves with:** the new word, scar, object or skill carried forward.
- **Script:** the cutscenes and in-play moments, in screenplay form.

**How to read the script**

- The game is wordless during play. Ichigo speaks only the few words they know, shown in play as a sound plus an icon bubble; the script writes the sound and puts the icon in brackets, e.g. ICHIGO: "Kaa-chan?" \[icon: a woman's face\].
- Other characters speak in pictogram bubbles, because Ichigo can't understand most of what adults say. The script gives what the pictograms *mean* in italics; the player only sees the pictures.
- SFX and MUSIC cues are for Zoe Cadogan-style sparse scoring: a single melody (the **Lullaby**) is introduced in Level 1 and recurs, changing as Ichigo grows.
- Ichigo is they/them throughout (the Cellar Door design).

## Level 1: The Great Wave

**Card:** 一 · 1 / 15 · Age 3 · *Odyssey* echo: the shipwreck (and *Twelfth Night*)

**Game type:** side-scrolling platformer on floating, rocking wreckage (built; see the design doc §3.1).

**Goal:** cross the open sea from the ruined beach to a small island with a stone lantern.

**Controls:** move, run, jump (hold for higher). No tools yet; the bucket hangs from Ichigo's wrist.

**Stakes:** decks tilt and are slippery, so standing still slides Ichigo into the sea. Straw mats sink. Waves grow as you travel. Falling in = a new tomorrow at the last carp streamer.

**Beats**

1. Prologue on the beach (interactive): walk to Kaa-chan, dig a hole, fill the bucket. Teaches movement with nothing at stake.
2. The wave arrives. A short unplayable shot, then Ichigo is in the water.
3. Gentle swells, wide steady planks and crates. Seven carp-streamer checkpoints.
4. Mid-level: drifting planks and straw mats, waves growing.
5. The oshiokuri boat as a breathing place, then the 150 px running jump that a walking jump misses on purpose.
6. Small planks that tip you off in about 2 seconds, then the island.

**What Ichigo leaves with:** the word *Kaa-chan*; a cut on the chin under a strawberry plaster (it becomes a faint white scar that lasts the whole game); the yellow bucket and red shovel; the Lullaby.

### Script

**TITLE.** The Great Wave curls left to right across warm paper. A small Fuji in the trough. Three oshiokuri boats. The rainbow-stacked title: ICHIGO. Below it, small: *A Child of the Sea.* PRESS ANY KEY.

**EXT. FISHING VILLAGE BEACH — LATE AFTERNOON**

Wide shot. Thatched wooden houses above a crescent of sand. Nets drying on poles. Carp streamers flap from one rooftop. On a bench by the boats, OJII-SAN, the old net-maker, bald with enormous eyebrows, dozes in the sun.

ICHIGO, three, bowl haircut, red jersey #15 worn to the knees, wooden flip-flops, sits in the sand with a yellow bucket and a red shovel. They dig.

KAA-CHAN, Ichigo's mother, mends a net on the steps of the house. She hums. MUSIC: the **Lullaby**, five notes rising and two falling, on a single wooden flute.

Player control begins. Ichigo walks in Sadie's walk: a few awkward sideways steps, speeding up, then forward.

If the player walks Ichigo toward the water, Ichigo trips over the shovel. A small *thup*. Ichigo sits up. A red dot on the chin. Their lip wobbles.

Kaa-chan is already there. She kneels, takes a plaster printed with strawberries from her apron and presses it on Ichigo's chin. She taps Ichigo's nose.

**KAA-CHAN:** \[pictogram: a strawberry, then a heart\] *My little strawberry.*

**ICHIGO:** "Kaa-chan." \[icon: a woman's face\]

She goes back to the net. (If the player never walks toward the water, Ichigo trips on the way back to the bucket instead. The cut always happens.)

The player fills the bucket at the water's edge and pours it into the hole. On the third pour, the water in the hole drains away by itself. The sea has pulled back. Fish flap on the bare sand. Ichigo looks up, delighted.

SFX: the gulls stop.

Kaa-chan stands. The net slides off her lap. She looks past Ichigo at the horizon and drops the needle.

**KAA-CHAN:** \[pictogram: Ichigo's face, an arrow, the house\] *Come here. Now.*

The player can run. It doesn't matter. The camera pulls back and the Great Wave fills the left of the screen, claws of foam reaching right. It rises over the roofs.

CUT TO WHITE. Silence for two seconds.

**EXT. OPEN SEA — DUSK**

Ichigo surfaces beside a floating plank, the bucket still on their wrist, and hauls themselves onto it. The village is gone. Wreckage in every direction: planks, crates, roof pieces, straw mats. Far off, a small island with a stone lantern.

**ICHIGO:** "Kaa-chan?" \[icon: a woman's face, small and grey\]

No answer. A carp streamer floats past, still bright.

Play begins.

**IN PLAY**

- First time Ichigo slides on a tilted deck: they throw their arms out. No text.
- First fall: fade to paper, a red sun rises over the sea, one tally mark appears in the corner. MUSIC: the first two notes of the Lullaby, every tomorrow.
- Reaching the boat: Ichigo sits down for a moment and holds the bucket. If the player waits five seconds, Ichigo looks back the way they came.

**EXT. LANTERN ISLAND — NIGHT**

Ichigo crawls onto black rock beside a stone lantern. They curl around the bucket. The great wave sits on the far horizon, small now.

Card: 一 · 1 / 15.

## Level 2: The Tide Comes In

**Card:** 二 · 2 / 15 · Age 3 · *Odyssey* echo: leaving home behind

**Game type:** sand-wall defense, seen from a high three-quarter view of one small island. A light tower-defense where the "towers" are walls and channels Ichigo digs by hand.

**Goal:** keep a dry patch of sand around the stone lantern until the tide turns at dawn, then run the sandbar to the next island before the tide comes back.

**Controls:** move; hold *dig* to heap sand into a wall in front of Ichigo; *dig* on wet sand cuts a channel that drains water away. The bucket carries one scoop of water out of a flooded patch.

**Stakes:** waves come in sets of three, each set higher. A wave breaks walls that are too low; channels lead water away but crumble after two sets. Crabs come out on a cycle and knock walls down. If water reaches Ichigo's dry patch, it's a new tomorrow at the start of that set.

**Beats**

1. Night 1, sets 1–3: learn walls, then channels.
2. Crabs appear between sets; Ichigo is light enough to step over them but walls aren't.
3. Sets 4–6: the island shrinks; Ichigo must give up the outer ring and wall a smaller patch.
4. Dawn: the tide turns. A sandbar rises out of the sea toward a green island.
5. Sandbar sprint (side view, short): the tide comes back from both sides; crabs burrow on a cycle; three-year-old Ichigo gets distracted by shells unless kept moving.

**What Ichigo leaves with:** the word *Umi*; a crab pinch on one finger; a cowrie shell they stopped for, if the player let them.

### Script

**EXT. LANTERN ISLAND — NIGHT**

Ichigo wakes curled against the stone lantern. Moonlight. The sea is close, closer each time the camera returns to it. A wave reaches Ichigo's flip-flops and pulls back. Ichigo pulls their feet in.

They look at the water, then at the bucket.

**ICHIGO:** "Umi." \[icon: three wave lines\]

It's the first time they've said it. They say it like the name of a big dog.

Play begins. MUSIC: a slow low drone that rises one step with each set of waves.

**IN PLAY**

- Between sets, a single crab comes up the sand, walks sideways along Ichigo's wall and knocks a gap in it. Ichigo copies its sideways walk for a few steps. (It is the same as their own walk.)
- First time Ichigo stands next to a crab for more than two seconds: it pinches their finger. Ichigo sucks the finger. A tiny red mark stays on the sprite.
- When the player gives up the outer ring of sand, Ichigo looks back at the abandoned wall as it melts.

**EXT. LANTERN ISLAND — DAWN**

The water slides away. A pale sandbar appears, running out to a green island with a mountain of trees. A red sun climbs behind it.

Ichigo stands. Brushes sand from their knees with very serious care. Picks up the bucket.

Sprint begins.

**EXT. SANDBAR — MORNING**

On the sandbar a cowrie shell shines. If the player lets Ichigo stop, they pick it up, hold it to their ear and drop it in the bucket. It costs two seconds. The tide is coming.

**EXT. GREEN ISLAND SHORE — MORNING**

Ichigo reaches dry sand just as the sandbar goes under behind them. They sit down hard. A warm, sweet smell: the camera tilts up to purple plum trees on the hill.

Card: 二 · 2 / 15.

## Level 3: The Sleepy Island

**Card:** 三 · 3 / 15 · Age 4 · *Odyssey* echo: the Lotus-eaters

**Game type:** point-and-click adventure across six connected screens of a warm island. Click to walk; click an object to use the bucket or shovel on it; drag items from the bucket (now Ichigo's inventory) onto people and places.

**Goal:** remember home, and wake someone who can help Ichigo leave.

**Stakes:** Ichigo is hungry, and the only easy food is the purple sleep-plums the castaways offer. A row of three memory icons sits in the corner: Kaa-chan's face, the house, the carp streamer. Each plum eaten fades one. If all three fade, Ichigo lies down under the trees and the next tomorrow begins on the shore. Ichigo gets distracted easily at this age: idle too long and they wander toward the plum trees.

**Beats**

1. The castaways: four adults asleep or half-asleep in hammocks, smiling, offering plums. None will get up.
2. Find other food: dig for clams at low tide; knock down a hard green citrus with the shovel.
3. Carrying water (new at age 4): a full bucket slows Ichigo and slops if they run. A splash of cold seawater wakes a castaway for a few seconds.
4. Memory puzzle: collect a roof tile, a piece of net and a scrap of red cloth, and lay them out on the sand to draw home. Each piece restores a faded memory icon.
5. With the drawing done, carry three buckets of seawater to the OLD SAILOR. Fully awake, he remembers the sea and points Ichigo to a log raft hidden in the reeds. Ichigo pushes off alone; he stays.

**What Ichigo leaves with:** the word *Uchi*; the habit of refusing the plums (if the player ate none, a small hidden badge on the save file); the sand drawing, which reappears in Level 11.

### Script

**EXT. GREEN ISLAND — PLUM GROVE — DAY**

Ichigo is a little taller. A new sprite: four years old, the jersey a bit shorter, the strawberry plaster gone and a pale line on the chin in its place.

Hammocks hang between the plum trees. A WOMAN IN A SUN HAT lies in one, eyes half shut, a plum in her hand. She holds it out.

**WOMAN:** \[pictogram: a plum, then a smiling closed eye\] *Eat. Rest. Nothing hurts here.*

Ichigo's stomach growls, an audible *grrrk*. They look at the plum for a long time.

Player control. If the player clicks the plum, Ichigo eats it. Juice runs down their chin. The colours on screen go one shade softer, and Kaa-chan's face in the corner fades to grey.

**IN PLAY**

- Clicking any castaway with an empty bucket: they wave a lazy hand. \[pictogram: a plum\] *Have one.*
- First splash of cold water: the castaway sits bolt upright, looks around, says \[pictogram: a ship, a question mark\] *Where's my ship?*, then sinks back down smiling.
- If Ichigo idles for ten seconds, they start walking toward the plum trees on their own. Any click stops them.

**EXT. GREEN ISLAND — SHORE — AFTERNOON**

The last piece of the sand drawing goes down: a roof of tile, a net for the door, a red cloth carp on a stick. Ichigo stands back.

**ICHIGO:** "Uchi." \[icon: a small house with a carp streamer\]

All three memory icons are bright. MUSIC: the Lullaby's opening notes, on the flute, once.

**EXT. REEDS — EVENING**

The third bucket hits the OLD SAILOR full in the face. He coughs, sits up and looks at Ichigo properly for the first time. He looks at the sand drawing behind them. His eyes are wet.

**OLD SAILOR:** \[pictogram: the house, a long arrow over waves, Ichigo\] *You still remember yours. Go.*

He parts the reeds. A raft of lashed logs. He pushes it into the water with Ichigo on it, then wades back toward the hammocks. Halfway there he picks up a plum. He looks at it. He drops it.

The current takes the raft. Ahead, on a dark point of rock, a lighthouse turns its single eye.

Card: 三 · 3 / 15.

## Level 4: The One-Eyed Lighthouse

**Card:** 四 · 4 / 15 · Age 4 · *Odyssey* echo: the Cyclops

**Game type:** stealth, side view across a rocky point, a boathouse and the lighthouse's three floors.

**Goal:** get the raft back from the KEEPER's boathouse and slip away before dawn.

**Controls:** move, run, jump; *hide* under the upturned bucket (only while standing still), crouch into gaps only a four-year-old fits through.

**Stakes:** the lighthouse beam sweeps the rocks on a fixed cycle. The Keeper patrols with a hand lantern; his one eye means he sees only on his right side, so his left is the safe side. Caught = he carries Ichigo back to his storeroom of washed-up things, and the next tomorrow starts there. Running is loud on rock; walking is quiet.

**Beats**

1. Arrival: the Keeper finds Ichigo, "keeps" them in his storeroom with the other lost things (a teapot, a doll, a ship's bell, dozens of sandals).
2. Out through a gap under the storeroom door (scrapes the elbow).
3. The rocks: beam cycles, tide pools that splash if you land in them, gulls that cry if startled.
4. The boathouse: the Keeper is asleep inside, snoring; the raft is tied with a knot Ichigo can't untie, so they must cut it with the shovel edge in three quiet goes between snores.
5. Push off. The escape run: the Keeper wakes and the beam locks onto the water. Paddle with the shovel out of its reach.

**What Ichigo leaves with:** the word *Ichigo*; a scraped elbow; a gap at the base of the lighthouse only a small child fits through (in a later optional return at age 7, it's too small, but the cliff above is climbable).

### Script

**EXT. LIGHTHOUSE POINT — NIGHT**

The raft grinds onto black rock. Above, the lighthouse turns. Its beam passes over Ichigo, stops, comes back.

A door opens at the base. The KEEPER steps out: huge in an oilskin coat, one eye, the other covered by a leather patch. He crouches. He looks at Ichigo the way you'd look at a good piece of driftwood.

**KEEPER:** \[pictogram: a question mark over a face\] *And what are you called?*

Ichigo opens their mouth. Nothing comes. They don't have the word yet.

The Keeper nods as if that settles it, picks Ichigo up under one arm, and the raft under the other.

**INT. STOREROOM — NIGHT**

Shelves to the ceiling: things the sea brought. A porcelain doll. A ship's bell. Hundreds of single sandals. He sets Ichigo on a shelf between a teapot and a lantern, pats their head and goes upstairs. A lock turns.

Ichigo looks at the doll. The doll looks back.

Player control. The gap under the door is just big enough.

**IN PLAY**

- Squeezing through the first gap: a scrape and a wince. A small mark appears on the elbow and stays.
- If the beam catches Ichigo, it stops on them. A pause. Heavy footsteps. Fade to the storeroom shelf, and the doll is now facing the door.
- Hiding under the bucket: only Ichigo's flip-flops show. If the Keeper passes on his blind side, he steps right over them.
- In the boathouse, the Keeper sleeps holding a single small red sandal.

**EXT. BOATHOUSE SLIP — BEFORE DAWN**

The last rope parts. The raft slides into black water. Ichigo paddles with the shovel. Behind them a lantern swings and the Keeper's shape fills the boathouse door.

**KEEPER:** \[pictogram: the question mark over a face, larger\] *What are you called?*

Ichigo stands up on the raft. They put both hands on their chest.

**ICHIGO:** "Ichigo!" \[icon: a strawberry\]

It echoes off the rocks. The Keeper goes still. Then he roars, a sound with no pictogram at all, and the great beam swings down off the horizon and onto the water, hunting.

The beam touches the sea and the sea answers. Wind. The first big swell lifts the raft.

Card: 四 · 4 / 15.

## Level 5: The Bag of Winds

**Card:** 五 · 5 / 15 · Age 5 · *Odyssey* echo: Aeolus

**Game type:** storm-survival arcade on a single raft, side view, one fixed screen that pitches and rolls. This is the game's showpiece for water, light and wind (milestone M7).

**Goal:** keep the raft afloat and Ichigo on it through three phases of a storm, about four minutes in all.

**Controls:** move along the raft to shift its balance; *grab* the mast rope to ride out a gust; *bail* with the bucket when water pools on the deck; *jump* over loose cargo sliding across.

**Stakes:** three meters, shown only as pictures on the raft itself, not as HUD bars: how low the raft sits (water on deck), how far it tilts, and whether Ichigo is holding on. Lightning flashes show the next big wave a second before it hits. Swept off = a new tomorrow at the start of the phase.

**Beats**

1. Calm: the WIND-KEEPER's island, a gift, a fair west wind. Home on the horizon.
2. Ichigo opens the bag.
3. Phase 1, Gusts: wind from changing sides; grab the rope or be blown off.
4. Phase 2, Rain: water fills the deck; bail between gusts, while crates slide.
5. Phase 3, The big sea: lightning shows each wave; run uphill on the raft as it climbs, then grab the rope at the crest. The last wave breaks the raft.
6. Ichigo swims (new at age 5) a short distance through the wreck to a dark hull.

**What Ichigo leaves with:** the word *Kaze*; a rope burn round one wrist; and a loss: the red shovel goes into the sea in the last wave.

### Script

**EXT. WIND-KEEPER'S ISLAND — MORNING**

A round island no bigger than a house, with one bent pine. Hundreds of cloth bags hang from its branches, each tied with coloured cord, each moving on its own.

The WIND-KEEPER, a tiny old woman with white hair blowing straight up, sits under the pine. She looks at Ichigo's raft, then at the scorch mark the lighthouse beam left on it. She clicks her tongue.

She takes down a fat grey bag tied with red cord and puts it in Ichigo's arms. It's almost as big as they are. It wriggles.

**WIND-KEEPER:** \[pictogram: the bag, a cord, a crossed-out hand\] *All the wrong winds are in here. Don't open it.*

She licks a finger, holds it up, and blows gently on the raft's little sail. It fills.

**EXT. OPEN SEA — MIDDAY**

The raft runs smoothly. MUSIC: the Lullaby, faster, almost happy. On the horizon a small grey cone appears: Fuji. Below it, a line of roofs.

Ichigo stands up. They take the cowrie shell out of the bucket and look at it. They look at the roofs. A present for Kaa-chan needs a bag.

They untie the red cord.

**ICHIGO:** "Kaze..." \[icon: three curling lines\]

The bag explodes. The sky goes from cream to slate in one frame. Fuji vanishes.

Play begins.

**IN PLAY**

- Each gust is drawn as a Hokusai claw of wind reaching across the screen before it hits, so the player can read it.
- First time Ichigo grabs the rope during a gust, the rope pulls tight around their wrist. A red mark appears and stays.
- Lightning: one white frame, everything shown in silhouette, the next wave visible in outline.
- If the player keeps the cowrie shell safe in the bucket through Phase 2, it rattles with every lurch.

**EXT. STORM — THE LAST WAVE**

A lightning flash shows a wave taller than the mast. Ichigo holds the rope with both hands. The wave breaks over them. The raft comes apart into planks.

Underwater: muffled, blue-black. The red shovel turns slowly as it sinks past Ichigo's face. They reach for it. It's gone.

Above, light. They swim.

**EXT. SHIP GRAVEYARD — DAWN**

Ichigo drags themselves onto the slanted deck of a long-dead ship. All around, masts stick up out of still grey water like a burned forest. The bucket is still on their wrist. The cowrie is still in it.

Card: 五 · 5 / 15.

## Level 6: Ship Graveyard

**Card:** 六 · 6 / 15 · Age 5 · *Odyssey* echo: none; the first stop the sea invents on its own

**Game type:** underwater exploration, side view, one interconnected map of sunken hulls (a small metroidvania). Above water, Ichigo walks slanted decks; below, they swim.

**Goal:** find a new shovel and dig out the one passage through the reef, under the ribs of a whale skeleton.

**Controls:** walk and jump on decks; *dive* off any edge; swim in eight directions; *surface* in air pockets inside hulls to breathe.

**Stakes:** short swims only (new at age 5): a breath bubble over Ichigo's head shrinks; reach air or it's a new tomorrow at the last air pocket. Moray eels lunge from portholes on a cycle. Chains sway and pin you if you swim under them as they fall. Some portholes Ichigo could have squeezed through at three are now too small, so the map forces a longer route.

**Beats**

1. The surface: masts and decks; learn to dive, find the first air pocket.
2. Meet the OLD TURTLE, who swims a few lengths ahead and waits. Following her is optional and reveals air pockets.
3. The deep hold: dark; Ichigo can only see a small circle around them. Eels.
4. The whale: a skeleton the length of three ships lying across the reef. Its shoulder blade, worn smooth, works as a shovel.
5. Dig out the sand under the ribs in three dives, each between breaths, while the current pushes back.
6. Through the ribs into open blue water. The turtle carries Ichigo on her back toward a green shadow under the sea.

**What Ichigo leaves with:** the word *Mite!*; a barnacle scrape on the shin; the whale-bone shovel, which replaces the red one for the rest of the game.

### Script

**EXT. SHIP GRAVEYARD — MORNING**

Ichigo is five now: longer legs, the jersey to just above the knee, the rope mark pale on the wrist.

They stand on a tilted deck and look down into still water. Through it, far below, the shapes of more ships. They hold their nose with one hand, the way they've seen bigger children do, and jump.

Player control. Underwater the sound drops to a hum. MUSIC: one low note held, very quiet.

**IN PLAY**

- First air pocket: Ichigo surfaces inside a hull with a gasp that echoes. A lantern of trapped light from a crack above.
- Squeezing at a porthole that's too small: Ichigo pushes, can't fit, frowns at their own shoulders.
- Brushing a hull: barnacles scrape the shin. A small mark stays.
- The OLD TURTLE, enormous, slow, with a shell crusted in coral, swims into frame and turns one eye toward Ichigo. She swims on. She waits.

**EXT. WHALE REEF — UNDERWATER**

The whale skeleton lies across the reef like a gate. Its shoulder blade has broken free and lies in the sand, flat and curved, like a big pale spoon.

Ichigo picks it up. It's heavier than the old shovel, and longer than their arm. They turn to the turtle and hold it up with both hands.

**ICHIGO:** "Mite!" \[icon: an open eye with a sparkle\]

Bubbles come out with the word. The turtle blinks slowly.

Digging begins.

**EXT. OPEN SEA — UNDERWATER — LATER**

The last of the sand slides away. The current through the ribs reverses and pulls Ichigo through into open blue. The turtle rises under them and they grab the edge of her shell.

She swims down, not up. Ahead, a forest of tall kelp sways, and lights glint between the stalks.

Card: 六 · 6 / 15.

## Level 7: The Kelp Garden

**Card:** 七 · 7 / 15 · Age 6 · *Odyssey* echo: Circe

**Game type:** physics puzzle, side view, eight rooms of a half-flooded garden built in a lagoon. Each room is one puzzle of ropes, pulleys, floats, levers and water.

**Goal:** open the tide-gate of each tank in the GARDENER's garden to free the creatures she keeps, then reach her shell house.

**Controls:** move, jump, swim; *pull* ropes and levers (new at age 6); fill and pour the bucket as a counterweight; tie kelp rope to posts and floats.

**Stakes:** the tide rises in every room on a clock, so a room that is easy at low water becomes a swim at high water, and some levers end up out of reach. The Gardener drifts through rooms, singing. If Ichigo stays in her sight too long, webbing creeps between their fingers; a full set of fins is a new tomorrow at the room's door.

**Beats**

1. The garden's edge: kelp rope and a simple pulley; a float lifts a gate.
2. Rooms 2–4: counterweights using the bucket, ropes over beams, a lever that only works when the room has flooded.
3. The creatures: in each tank, a fish, a crab, an octopus, a seal; each was once a lost sailor, and each looks at Ichigo with a person's eyes.
4. Rooms 5–8: freed creatures help in the next room (the octopus holds a rope; the seal pushes a float).
5. The shell house: the Gardener, and a choice that isn't one: stay safe as a fish, or go.

**What Ichigo leaves with:** the word *Arigatou*; a paper lantern; a coil of kelp rope; the Gardener's direction, which leads into Level 8.

### Script

**EXT. KELP GARDEN — LAGOON — DAY**

The turtle lets Ichigo off in waist-deep water, where the kelp breaks the surface in neat rows like a rice field. Stone tanks rise out of the water, each with a gate of woven reeds. Behind them, on stilts, a house made of a single enormous shell.

Ichigo is six: longer face, missing a front tooth, the jersey now a short tunic.

## Level 8: Lantern Cave

**Card:** 八 · 8 / 15 · Age 6 · *Odyssey* echo: the underworld (Odysseus meets his mother's ghost)

**Game type:** quiet light-puzzle adventure. Row the reed boat through flooded cave channels (top-down), and climb out onto ledges to solve light puzzles (side view). No enemies.

**Goal:** follow the lantern that hums the Lullaby to the cave's heart.

**Controls:** row (left and right oars, so turning is a skill); on foot, move, jump, carry and set down the lantern; push shell mirrors to turn them; guide floating lanterns with the oar.

**Stakes:** darkness. The cave is lit only by Ichigo's lantern, which burns down over time. It can be relit from any lantern of the dead that has reached its alcove. If it goes out, everything goes black, and the next tomorrow begins at the last lit alcove. Touching the water with the lantern puts it out.

**Beats**

1. The mouth: hundreds of small floating lanterns drift deeper. Each is the light of someone the sea took.
2. Channels: row upstream against a slow current; turn with one oar.
3. Alcoves: steer drifting lanterns into empty niches in the wall; each lit niche opens the way and gives a relight.
4. The mirror hall: turn shell mirrors to bounce one beam across the cave and light a far shore.
5. The humming lantern: Ichigo follows it, sure it's Kaa-chan.
6. The heart: the lantern is OJII-SAN, the old net-maker from Ichigo's village.

**What Ichigo leaves with:** the word *Sayonara*; the ability to hum the Lullaby (a new button, needed in Level 9); the guiding star; and the knowledge that Kaa-chan isn't here.

### Script

**EXT. CAVE MOUTH — NIGHT**

The reed boat drifts in under the rock. Water drips. All around, floating paper lanterns, each with a small flame, drift slowly inward on the current. Some are big. Some are very small.

Ichigo holds their own lantern up. The others don't seem to notice it.

Player control.

**IN PLAY**

- First lantern guided into its alcove: its flame brightens, the paper shows for a moment a face (a fisherman, a girl, a dog), and then it settles, calm.
- When Ichigo's lantern gets low, the flame shrinks and the edges of the screen close in. Ichigo holds it closer to their face.

Ichigo rows to it, fast, oars splashing.

**ICHIGO:** "Sayonara." \[icon: a waving hand\]

## Level 9: Singing Rocks

**Card:** 九 · 9 / 15 · Age 7 · *Odyssey* echo: the Sirens

**Game type:** rhythm game. The reed boat rows forward on its own track through a maze of rocks; the player keeps it moving and on course by rowing in time and humming the true Lullaby over the false one.

**Goal:** row through the Singing Rocks to open water without wrecking the boat.

**Controls:** left oar and right oar on the beat (alternating = straight, doubling one side = turn); *hum* (learned in Level 8) held across the Lullaby's phrases.

**Stakes:** the rocks sing the Lullaby in Kaa-chan's voice, but slightly wrong: some notes late, one note changed. Follow their beat and the boat drifts toward them, and the steering itself pulls off course. Hum the true Lullaby on the true beat and their voices fade for a phrase. The boat can take three hits on the rocks; the third sinks it, and the next tomorrow begins at the last marker buoy.

**Beats**

1. Tying on: Ichigo ties themselves to the seat with the kelp rope, so a pull can't drag them overboard.
2. Open channel: rowing rhythm alone, to learn the beat.
3. The first singer: a cormorant on a rock, singing in Kaa-chan's voice. The beat slips. The player learns to hum against it.
4. The chorus: many rocks, many voices, now including Tou-chan calling Ichigo's name. Two wrong rhythms at once.
5. The last rock: the voice says Ichigo's name exactly as Kaa-chan did on the beach. Ichigo answers.

**What Ichigo leaves with:** the word *Iya!*; the boat, scraped but whole.

### Script

**EXT. SINGING ROCKS — MORNING**

Ichigo is seven: a growth spurt, all knees and elbows. The jersey is a shirt now, the 15 stretched across a narrow chest.

Dozens of low black rocks stand out of a flat grey sea. On each one sits a black cormorant with its wings hung out to dry. All of them face Ichigo.

Faintly, from every rock at once: the Lullaby, in a woman's voice.

Ichigo stands up in the boat. Their face opens. Then they frown. They hum a phrase back. The rocks' version has one note wrong.

Ichigo sits down, takes the kelp rope and ties a knot around their waist and the boat's seat. They pull it tight.

Play begins. MUSIC: the Lullaby, but the player can now hear two versions braided together.

**IN PLAY**

- When the player follows the false beat, the cormorants lean closer, and the true colours of the sea drain to a warm, sleepy gold.
- When the player hums the true phrase, the nearest cormorant stops singing and just looks like a bird again.
- Each hit on a rock: a crack, a jolt, the knot holds.
- Halfway through, a man's voice joins in from the rocks, deeper, calling Ichigo's name. It's the first time the game has had Tou-chan's voice. Ichigo has never heard it in-game. The player will hear it again in Level 15.

**EXT. THE LAST ROCK — NOON**

One tall rock, and on it one bird, larger than the rest. It sings, and then it speaks, in Kaa-chan's voice:

**THE VOICE:** \[pictogram: Ichigo's face, an arrow, the rock\] *Come here. Now.*

The same pictogram, the same words, as on the beach before the wave.

Ichigo grips the oars. Their knuckles go white.

**ICHIGO:** "Iya!" \[icon: a crossed-out hand\]

They row, hard and on the true beat, past the rock. The bird flaps once and becomes only a bird. The singing stops. Silence, and then real gulls.

**EXT. OPEN SEA — AFTERNOON**

Ichigo unties the knot. Ahead, the sea narrows between two cliffs. Between them, the water turns slowly, in a great circle.

## Level 10: Whirlpool Strait

**Card:** 十 · 10 / 15 · Age 7 · *Odyssey* echo: Scylla and Charybdis

**Game type:** two halves. First, a top-down current-reading run in the boat. Then a vertical climbing action level up a sea cliff, with a grip meter.

**Goal:** get through the strait. The boat won't make it; Ichigo will.

**Controls:** Part 1: row, and read the foam lines that show each current. Part 2: climb (new at age 7) by grabbing holds; *hang* to rest the grip; *jump* between holds; swim against weak currents (new at age 7) at the end.

**Stakes:** Part 1: the whirlpool's pull grows each lap; if the boat reaches the centre, it's a new tomorrow at the strait's mouth. Part 2: grip drains while climbing and refills while hanging from good holds. Arms of a giant octopus reach out of cliff caves on a cycle; a hit knocks Ichigo off. Falls drop Ichigo to the last ledge with a carp streamer.

**Beats**

1. The strait: the whirlpool on the left, a cliff full of caves on the right. Row the currents along the cliff side.
2. The pull wins. As the boat tips into the circle, Ichigo leaps for the cliff.
3. The climb: three stretches, each with octopus caves; hang on solid holds to rest.
4. The arm: one tentacle catches Ichigo's forearm and drags. The scene that leaves the big scar.
5. The pine: near the top, Ichigo hangs from a pine root over the whirlpool and waits, as Odysseus did, for the sea to give the boat back. It comes back up as planks.
6. Drop onto a plank, swim it against the weakening current to a beach.

**What Ichigo leaves with:** the word *Itai*; a long scar down the left forearm (the most visible mark Ichigo will carry); and a loss: the reed boat.

Two cliffs close in. On the left, the sea turns in a slow wide circle, its centre a dark funnel; it breathes in, and the whole strait leans toward it. On the right, a cliff riddled with black caves.

## Level 11: The Fishermen's Island

**Game type:** survival crafting over five in-game days, side view across one long island with a day-night cycle.

**Goal:** stay fed and build a raft before the fishing boats come back on the sixth morning.

**Controls:** move, climb, swim; *gather* (driftwood, reeds, shells, seaweed); *dig* (clams, fresh water at the foot of the dunes); *build* (new at age 8) at a work spot to make a shelter, a fire, a sand wall, and a raft in four stages.

## Level 12: The Kind House

**Card:** 十二 · 12 / 15 · Age 8 → 9 · *Odyssey* echo: Calypso

**Game type:** a small life-sim through one year in four seasons, side view of a house, a garden, a dock and a cove. Each day is a short chore minigame; each evening is a choice.

**Goal:** by the next spring, have both a finished raft (hidden in the cove) and OBAA-SAN's blessing (shown as the sail she weaves).

**Controls:** each chore has its own simple control: scatter feed for chickens, time a pole-knock to drop persimmons, stir the indigo vat, thread a net needle, follow brush strokes to write. In the evening, choose: sit with Obaa-san, or slip down to the cove and work on the raft.

**Stakes:** there are only 12 evenings in the year. Evenings at the cove build the raft. Evenings with Obaa-san teach Ichigo to write and fill the indigo sail on her loom. Both have to be finished by spring. If either isn't, another season passes (the screen fades through a full turn of the year) and the player gets four more evenings: this level's version of a new tomorrow. Chores can be failed and retried freely.

**Beats**

1. Summer: Ichigo wakes in a clean futon. Chickens, a persimmon tree, the indigo vat. Obaa-san's house has one empty place at the table and a photo of a young man in a fisherman's coat.
2. Autumn: persimmons; mending nets (Ichigo does it the way Kaa-chan did, before Obaa-san shows them).
3. Winter: writing lessons by the brazier; the raft hidden under snow. Obaa-san finds it.
4. Spring: Obaa-san's strawberries ripen. Ichigo turns nine. She gives them the sail.

**What Ichigo leaves with:** the word *Mata ne*; the ability to write their own name; the indigo sail; and nine years.

Ichigo wakes in a real bed for the first time in five years. Sunlight through paper screens. The smell of rice.

OBAA-SAN kneels by the futon with a bowl. She is very small, very old, and her hands are blue to the wrist from indigo dye.

She looks at the scar on Ichigo's arm and says nothing. She puts the bowl in their hands.

**OBAA-SAN:** \[pictogram: the three characters, then a strawberry\] *That's you.*

## Level 14: The Last Storm

**Card:** 十四 · 14 / 15 · Age 9 → 10 · *Odyssey* echo: Poseidon's last storm, Odysseus washed ashore

**Game type:** a return to the Level 1 platformer, on rocking wreckage in a storm, now with everything Ichigo has learned. Same engine, same art, same carp streamers: the player should feel how much has changed.

**Goal:** cross the storm from the wreck of the raft to the coast under Fuji.

**Controls:** Level 1's move, run and jump, plus: grab ropes and masts in gusts (Level 5), short swims between pieces with a breath bubble (Level 6), climb up the sides of capsized hulls (Level 10).

**Stakes:** Ichigo is heavy now. The same small planks that barely moved under a three-year-old tip hard and sink under a nine-year-old, so the player has to choose bigger pieces and keep moving. In return, Ichigo runs faster and jumps much farther. Gusts come as Hokusai claws of wind; lightning shows the next wave a second ahead. Falling in can be survived with a short swim, if there's a piece close enough; otherwise it's a new tomorrow at the last carp streamer.

**Beats**

1. The raft breaks up. The indigo sail tears loose and wraps round a floating mast; Ichigo keeps a strip of it.
2. Planks and crates, as in Level 1, but they tip. Learn the new weight.
3. A drifting fishing fleet's wreckage: masts with ropes to grab in the gusts.
4. Capsized hulls to climb; swims between them.
5. Lightning section: every wave shown only in flashes.
6. The Great Wave. It doesn't take Ichigo away; it lifts them toward land. A short, almost automatic run along its face, under the claws.

**What Ichigo leaves with:** the word *Kaa-chan!*, in an older voice; a torn jersey; ten years; and a loss: the yellow bucket.

### Script

**EXT. OPEN SEA — STORM**

The white line was a wave. It hits the raft. Planks fly.

Ichigo comes up beside a plank, exactly as in Level 1: same frame, same composition. But this Ichigo is nine, long-limbed, short-haired, scarred, the jersey ragged at the hem, and when they pull themselves onto the plank it dips under their weight in a way it never did before.

They look at the plank. They look at their own hands.

The indigo sail flaps past on a broken mast. Ichigo grabs a corner and it tears. They tie the strip round their wrist, over the old rope scar.

Play begins. MUSIC: the Lullaby, full, for the first time with drums.

**IN PLAY**

- The first time Ichigo lands on a small plank: it tips sharply and they slide. A player used to Level 1 will fall. That is the point.
- Carp streamers mark checkpoints, as in Level 1. Some are torn and faded, as if they've been floating for years.
- Ichigo still throws their arms out to balance, the same pose as at three.
- After a fall, at sunrise: the tally of tomorrows has carried over from Level 1. It's long now.

**EXT. STORM — THE GREAT WAVE**

Lightning. The last flash shows it: the Great Wave, enormous, its lip a row of claws, exactly as on the title screen and the beach seven years ago. It's behind Ichigo this time. In front of them, through the rain: a dark coast, and Fuji.

The wave lifts the wreckage Ichigo is standing on. The run along its face is almost automatic: the player only has to keep running. The claws reach over Ichigo's head and don't close.

At the crest, the yellow bucket slips off Ichigo's wrist. They grab for it. They miss. It tumbles down the face of the wave with the cowrie shell inside.

Ichigo watches it go. Then they turn and face the coast.

**ICHIGO:** "Kaa-chan!" \[icon: a woman's face\]

The voice is a ten-year-old's. The same word as in the first scene of the game.

White.

## Level 15: Home

**Card:** 十五 · 15 / 15 · Age 10 · *Odyssey* echo: the homecoming, the scar

**Game type:** a short walking narrative, side view, through the rebuilt village. No hazards, no fails. The only verbs are walking, running and saying the words Ichigo has learned.

**Goal:** find home. Then stand still.

**Controls:** move, run (Ichigo runs "like a wave", full speed, foam at their heels even on dry land); a word wheel holding every word Ichigo has learned, any of which they can say to anyone.

**Stakes:** none that can fail. The level's one rule reverses the whole game. Since Level 1, standing still on the sea meant sliding off. Here, the ending only happens when the player lets Ichigo stand still and be looked at.

**Beats**

1. The beach: the same crescent, new houses. Ichigo is too big for the beach toys a small child has left in the sand.
2. The quay: people mending nets. Nobody knows the scarred stranger. Every word on the wheel gets a polite, puzzled pictogram back.
3. Ojii-san's bench, empty, with a stone lantern beside it.
4. The house: new, in the same place, with carp streamers. Through the open door, the household shrine: a photo of a three-year-old in a red jersey, and in front of it, a red shovel the sea brought back.
5. Kaa-chan comes out. She doesn't know them.
6. Stand still.

**What Ichigo leaves with:** *Tadaima*.

### Script

**EXT. VILLAGE BEACH — MORNING**

Ichigo is ten. Taller than the carp-streamer poles were at three. The strip of indigo round the wrist, the whale-bone shovel in their belt, the jersey just a shirt now with a faded 15.

The beach is the same crescent. The houses above it are new wood, roofed in grey tile where there used to be thatch.

In the sand, a small child's yellow bucket and spade, abandoned. Ichigo crouches beside them. They pick up the spade. It's tiny in their hand. They put it back exactly where it was.

Player control. MUSIC: none. Only the sea, gulls, and a net-maker's mallet somewhere.

**IN PLAY**

- Running: Ichigo runs like a wave, with a curl of foam behind them on the dry sand. Villagers turn to look.
- Saying a word to a villager: they answer kindly and don't understand. *Umi* gets a nod at the sea. *Ichigo* gets a smile and a pictogram of a strawberry, because it's a word, not a name, to them. *Kaa-chan* to a woman makes her look around for the child's mother.
- *If Ichigo took nothing on the Fishermen's Island:* the CAPTAIN is on the quay, unloading. She stops. She knows the child from the island and the drawing. She puts down her crate and walks a few steps behind Ichigo the rest of the way, saying nothing.
- *If Ichigo took fish:* the Captain is on the quay. She looks at Ichigo, and looks away.
- Ojii-san's bench: empty, with a small stone lantern beside it. If Ichigo sits, they hum the Lullaby, once, without the player pressing anything.

**EXT. ICHIGO'S HOUSE — LATE MORNING**

A new house on the old spot. Carp streamers on a pole, faded. A net hanging to dry.

Through the open door: a household shrine. A photo of a small child in a red jersey with a bucket. In front of it, a red plastic shovel, sun-bleached and scratched. The one the sea took in Level 5.

Ichigo stops on the step.

KAA-CHAN comes round the corner of the house with a basket of washing. She's seven years older. A grey streak in her hair. She sees a ragged, scarred, sunburned stranger child on her step, and her face does what Obaa-san's did: worry, then kindness.

**KAA-CHAN:** \[pictogram: a bowl, a cup of water, a cloth\] *You poor thing. Come, eat. Let me wash your face.*

She doesn't know them.

The player can move Ichigo away. If they do, Kaa-chan calls after the stranger once, kindly, and goes on hanging the washing. The scene waits.

The player can say any word on the wheel. *Kaa-chan* makes her smile sadly and shake her head: \[pictogram: the photo on the shrine\] *I had one your age, once.*

The ending happens only when the player stops moving Ichigo and leaves them still.

**EXT. ICHIGO'S HOUSE — THE STEP**

Ichigo stands still.

Kaa-chan kneels with the wet cloth, the way she knelt on the beach with the plaster. She wipes the salt off the stranger's face. Forehead. Cheeks. Chin.

Her thumb stops on the pale line on the chin.

She doesn't move. The cloth drips. She looks at the line, then at the shrine, then at the child's face, as if for the first time.

Slowly she presses her thumb on the scar, exactly where the strawberry plaster went.

Ichigo doesn't know why she has stopped. They say the only word they have left that they haven't said.

**ICHIGO:** "Tadaima." \[icon: a small house with an open door\]

Kaa-chan's face breaks.

**KAA-CHAN:** "Okaeri."

The only word any real person besides Ichigo says aloud in the whole game. (The voices at the Singing Rocks were not real.)

From down the path, a man's voice calling, the same deep voice as from the Singing Rocks, the real one now: TOU-CHAN, back from the boats, a net over his shoulder, stopping dead.

Wide shot. The house, the three of them, the carp streamers lifting in a wind off the sea. On the horizon, very small, the Great Wave, still. It doesn't move.

Title card, in the cover's rainbow lettering: ICHIGO.

Card: 十五 · 15 / 15.

## Credits and open questions

**Credits sequence.** The credits roll over a slow pan back across the whole voyage, right to left, as one long Hokusai seascape: the house, the storm, the raft's route, Obaa-san's dock, the fishing island, the strait, the cormorant rocks, the lantern cave, the kelp garden, the ship graveyard, the wind-keeper's pine, the lighthouse, the sleepy island, the lantern island, and finally open sea. MUSIC: the Lullaby as Ichigo hums it at ten, then as Kaa-chan hummed it at the start. Last image: the yellow bucket floating upright on a calm sea, the cowrie shell inside, drifting toward a small figure on a distant beach. The tally of tomorrows is shown once, with no comment.

**Open questions**

- **The recognition scar.** The design doc (§5) says a scar from an earlier level lets the family recognize Ichigo. This script makes it the chin cut from Level 1's prologue, because it's the only mark the parents could have seen. The forearm scar from Level 10 is the most visible one but means nothing to them. Update §5 to match?
- **Scope.** Fourteen game types is a lot of engines. Most can share the existing core (pixel pipeline, sea, wreckage, Ichigo's sprite, tomorrows). The ones needing the most new tech: Level 3 (point-and-click), Level 9 (rhythm), Level 12 (life-sim chores), Level 13 (top-down sailing). Should any be simplified to platformer variants?
- **Ichigo's words.** The 14 Japanese words and their icons should be checked by a native speaker for how a small child would actually say them.
- **The pictogram language.** Other characters speak only in pictograms. This needs its own small design pass: a fixed vocabulary of perhaps 40 symbols, reused across levels so players learn it.
- **The Fishermen's Island choice.** It currently changes only one moment in Level 15. Is that enough, or should it affect Level 13 (the Captain's boats passing) too?
- **Level 12's failure loop.** Is an extra season passing the right "new tomorrow" for a level where Ichigo can't die?
- **Sequel hook.** Leave out any Hanami (Ichigo II) hook so the ending stays whole, or add a post-credits beat?
